How Our Reseller Program Works

Dear Reseller,

Here’s how our community price system works:

  1. We will post the Community Price of each product in the group.
  2. You can add your own margin to this price and sell to your customer.
  3. Example:
    • Community Price = ₹650
    • You fix your margin = ₹100
    • Selling Price = ₹750
  4. When you get an order:
    • Collect ₹750 from your customer.
    • Keep your commission ₹100 (your profit).
    • Send ₹650 to us.
  5. After receiving the payment, we will dispatch the product directly to your customer.

📌 Note: You are free to decide your margin based on the market price of the product.
